InstantCash Android App

Built natively in Kotlin & Jetpack Compose with Room Database, Stripe Escrow, and PayPal Hyperwallet.

💸

Instant Payouts

Cash out earnings immediately to your debit card or bank account powered by PayPal Hyperwallet integration.

🛡️

Stripe Escrow Locking

Customer payments are locked securely in escrow as soon as a job is accepted to guarantee payment protection.

📍

GPS Check-In & Photo Proof

Workers check in via GPS and upload photo evidence upon completing local tasks for quick approval.

In-App Messaging & Disputes
